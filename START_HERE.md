## "I've been gone a while and I feel like I lost everything"

You didn't. Recognition comes back far faster than it built. Reloading is
hours, not weeks. Do this, in order:

1. **Read `cheatsheets/00_complexity.md` and `cheatsheets/_patterns.md`.**
   20 minutes. That's the whole map — every pattern, its trigger, its template.
2. **Run `./review`.** It tells you which topics have decayed the most.
3. **Re-solve ONE problem you've already solved before**, from a blank file.
   Not a new one. The point is to prove to yourself the knowledge is still in
   there. It is.
4. Only after that green test, go back to wherever `PROGRESS.md` left off.

Total: about 90 minutes to be functional again. Not a restart.

## Why this repo is built the way it is

The reason DSA knowledge evaporates is that it's stored as **3000 loose facts**
instead of **16 patterns**. Loose facts decay. Patterns compress, and compressed
things reload fast.

So every module here produces three artifacts, deliberately:

| Artifact | Purpose | Decays? |
|----------|---------|---------|
| `problems/` | the reps that build intuition | fast |
| `notes/` | the full explanation, for relearning | slowly |
| `cheatsheets/` | the compressed trigger + template | **barely** |

When you come back, you reload from the cheatsheet — the smallest, most durable
artifact — and the rest decompresses behind it. That's the whole design.

**The one rule:** when you finish a module, write its cheatsheet *in your own
words*. Not mine. The act of compressing it is what makes it stick, and future-
you will trust your own phrasing more than anyone else's.
